# Introduce functional scope governance

Projects need a governed functional-scope artefact that captures current business-domain boundaries and structural assumptions separately from requirements and architecture decisions.

The governance should define where the functional scope is stored, what it contains, how agents discover and maintain it, and how requirements are kept consistent with it. The artefact must describe the project's current scope without implying speculative future versions or roadmap states.

Open questions include whether the functional scope needs its own lifecycle or relationships, and how changes to established functional boundaries should be reviewed and recorded.
